Part 02 / The idea

An old art form for a robot we left behind.

Chinese shadow puppetry has three parts: a lit screen, a jointed puppet and the rods that move it. Shadows Left Behind keeps all three and adds one change. Your hand becomes the rod.

Part 07 / Honest storytelling

What really happened, and what we imagined.

History FACT

  • 2004: Opportunity lands on Mars for a planned primary mission of 90 Martian days.
  • Last Chance rock: fine layers at different angles in the rock suggest that water once flowed there.
  • 14+ years, ~45.16 km: how long it actually worked and how far it drove.
  • June 10, 2018: last communication, during a planet-wide dust storm.
  • February 13, 2019: NASA declares the mission complete.
  • The NASA photographs shown on stage come from the real mission archive.

Story FICTION

  • Oppy's first-person voice is written for the stage. It is not a real transmission.
  • The “awakening” is a theatre metaphor. Nothing restarts the real rover.
  • Meeting you, the memory keepsakes and the photo together are invented moments.
  • “Abandoned” answers the competition theme emotionally. The mission team never stopped caring.
  • The voice is neural text-to-speech, not an actor's recording.
